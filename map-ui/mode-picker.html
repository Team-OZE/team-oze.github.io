Game Setup
-PR: Random Prophet
You get a pool of random units to build each wave
-PH: Manual Prophet
You may re-randomise your pool of buildable units 6 times per game
-AP: All Pick
You pick your building pool, you may change your choice up to 5 times per game
-HB: Hybrid Builder
You may build a random tower of a selected tier for the average price
-CC: Challenge Champions
Each player may manually challenge a Creep Champion every level
-AC: Auto Champions
Creep Champions will spawn every wave after wave 4
-NC: No Champions
No Creep Champions will spawn
-X1: Normal Mode
Sends are not multiplied
-X2: Double Trouble
Sends are multiplied 2 times
-X3: Triple Threat
Sends are multiplied 3 times
-X4: Quadruple Chaos
Sends are multiplied 4 times
-AH: Allow Hybrid
Allow Hybrid Builder in PR/PH mode
 
-MI: Mirror Mode
The random build pools are the same across teams for players at the same positions
-EZ: Easy Mode
Wave damage is decreased, the King is more powerful
-HM: Hard Mode
Wave damage is increased
