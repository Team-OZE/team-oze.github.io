The Game

Legion TD is a Warcraft III custom game, where teams of players build units to defend their mighty Kings against waves of demonic creatures.
It features numerous gamemodes, such as different builder modes, varying difficulty levels and optional levels.

Play it in Warcraft III Reforged now! Just download the latest version from Reforged Maps and get started!
An overview of the units, waves and more is available in the Wiki

W3Champions - Legion TD Ladder

There is an official Legion TD ladder on W3Champions.
It currently features pracmi, phcc and prccx3 modes.

W3Champions is a secure environment to play Warcraft III: Reforged. Some of the platform's features include:

  • Verified and updated maps - no hacked maps that cause you to lose your stats and crash all the time.
  • A competitive ladder - a centralised ranking and a robust system for fair matchmaking. Server-stored stats make sure you will never lose your progress!
  • Moderation - no more pesky leavers! You can submit reports against offenses in your ladder games.
  • Statistics - games on the platform are recorded and further statistcs on your performance and general trends are available through the W3Champions website and, in the future, far more - on this website.
